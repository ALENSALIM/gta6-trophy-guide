      Missable trophies — an honest answer

      No trophy in this list is flagged as missable. That is deliberate. A trophy is only flagged missable here when its official description clearly ties it to a one-time story moment or choice, and none of the 55 official descriptions does that. Flagging one on a guess would be inventing a missable — and a wrong flag is worse than no flag, because it would send you rushing through the story for no reason.

      The trophies below are the ones to double-check once the game is out. They are a watchlist, not a missable list: each one is marked “not yet confirmed” and the guide says so next to the trophy itself.

        What this means in practice

        • Play the story in order and the chapter and named-mission trophies come on their own — they are story progress, not missables.
        • Missions in this series have traditionally been replayable, so a failed or finished mission is not a lost trophy. That is series history, not a confirmed fact about this game, so the watchlist stays until the game is out.
        • If a choice trophy turns out to lock behind one decision, this guide will say so — with a source — and only then will it be flagged.

        Sources & method

        • Names, descriptions and grades — the official GTA VI trophy list as published on Exophase. Every trophy in this guide uses the official name and description exactly.
        • PSN-tracked counts — PSNProfiles, cited beside a trophy where it tracks a count (for example, the count towards 500 on Actions Speak Louder).
        • Every guide is original — written from the official description for this guide. A step that has not yet been checked in the game is labelled as advice from the description, not as a verified fact. Nothing here is copied from another guide.
        • Missable policy — only what the official description clearly ties to a one-time story moment or choice is flagged. Nothing in this list meets that bar, so nothing is flagged, and the watchlist above names the trophies to re-check.
        • Privacy — your checklist is stored only in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Export and import use a file on your own device.
